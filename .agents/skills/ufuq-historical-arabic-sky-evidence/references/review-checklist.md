# Historical Arabic sky evidence review checklist

- [ ] The task explicitly invoked `$ufuq-historical-arabic-sky-evidence`.
- [ ] The investigation starts from a source-led question, not a predetermined list of
  names or patterns to confirm.
- [ ] One atomic claim facet is stated.
- [ ] Primary-source wording, secondary interpretation, Codex inference, and project
  decision are visibly separate.
- [ ] Canonical source ID, edition/witness, exact location, period, and source tradition
  are recorded when available.
- [ ] Exact source-language text already verified, transliteration system/output,
  translation source, and uncertainties are preserved.
- [ ] Attestation is one of `ATTESTED`, `UNVERIFIED`, or
  `NOT_FOUND_IN_REVIEWED_SCOPE`.
- [ ] Referent type is recorded independently.
- [ ] Modern-identification status is recorded independently.
- [ ] Geographic basis is recorded independently.
- [ ] Review status is recorded independently and Codex has not set
  `APPROVED_FOR_LESSON`.
- [ ] Name occurrence, referent, tradition, geography, modern identification,
  catalogue objects, exact membership, line drawing, directional use, and lesson
  approval are answered separately.
- [ ] Modern IDs have their own argument; coordinates remain in numerical catalogue
  artifacts.
- [ ] Alternate forms, competing interpretations, conflicts, OCR/edition limits, and
  translation uncertainty remain visible.
- [ ] `NOT_FOUND_IN_REVIEWED_SCOPE` is not represented as historical disproof.
- [ ] A contradiction is recorded only against a specific claim and cited source or
  scholarly analysis.
- [ ] General Arabic/Arabian evidence is not promoted to specifically Najdi evidence.
- [ ] Unresolved facets are excluded from learner-facing content without rejecting the
  broader historical possibility.
