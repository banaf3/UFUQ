## Claim-specific authority

- `IBN-QUTAYBA-ANWA`: primary historical attestation only at the inspected claim
  locations. Sections 31–32 (local discovery pages 68–69) and 52–53 (local discovery
  pages 80–82) require an edition-stable witness and human language review before
  learner-facing use.
- `KUNITZSCH-1961`: secondary philological analysis and conflict control. Relevant
  locations include entries 55–56 (printed p. 48), 107a (printed pp. 62–63), 136a–c
  (printed p. 72), and 148a–149 (printed p. 75). OCR, German, Arabic, and Greek details
  require page-image and human verification.
- `HAFEZ-2010`: secondary Greco-Arabic/al-Ṣūfī manuscript and modern crosswalk study.
  Relevant locations include thesis/PDF pp. 124–125, 133, and 151 and Tables 18, 19,
  24, and 26. Its HR identifiers are scholarly proposals, not catalogue coordinates or
  cultural approval.
- `KING-1993` and `KING-1999`: historical Islamic-astronomy and Qibla-method context.
  They are not authorities for star membership, modern catalogue identity, production
  geodesy, or regional sky use.
- `UFUQ-HISTORICAL-SKY-SCOPE`, `UFUQ-DATA`, and `UFUQ-ARCH`: project decisions that
  define source-led discovery, separated evidence facets, numerical/cultural
  separation, generic relationships, and lesson-review gates. They are not historical
  evidence.

The current reviewed source set does not establish specifically Najdi usage. That is a
reviewed-scope limitation, not historical disproof. `CULT-SRC-001` through
`CULT-SRC-004` in `docs/references/SOURCE_GAPS.md` remain relevant if UFUQ later makes
a specifically Najdi or learner-facing claim.

If local material is absent, unreadable, edition-unstable, or outside the inspected
scope, mark the affected facet `UNVERIFIED`, `UNKNOWN`, or `UNRESOLVED`; do not invent
a citation, translation, identification, or negative historical conclusion.
